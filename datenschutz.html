2. General information

We process personal data only to the extent necessary to provide our website and to handle your requests. Processing is carried out in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

3. Hosting and server log files

Our website is provided via GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit the website, GitHub processes technically necessary data, in particular:

  • IP address
  • date and time of access
  • page visited and referrer URL
  • browser type, browser version and operating system

This data is required to deliver the website and ensure its security. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a stable and secure website). Data may be transferred to the USA. GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; the transfer is based on the European Commission's adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR). More information: GitHub's privacy statement.

4. Contact form

When you write to us using the contact form, we process the information you provide (type of request, name, email address, institution, message) to handle your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your request relates to a contract or pre-contractual measures, and otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in responding to requests).

To transmit the form we use the form service Web3Forms (web3forms.com). Your information is sent to Web3Forms and forwarded to us by email. Data may be transferred to countries outside the EU; in that case the transfer is based on appropriate safeguards pursuant to Art. 46 GDPR, in particular the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

We process our emails using Microsoft 365 (Microsoft Ireland Operations Limited, Dublin, Ireland).

We delete your information once your request has been fully handled, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

5. Fonts

The font used on this website is loaded locally from our server. No connection is made to servers of Google or other third parties.

6. Local storage in your browser

If you change the language of the website, we store your choice in your browser's local storage (localStorage) so that the page appears in the same language on your next visit. This information does not leave your device and is not transmitted to us. Storing it is strictly necessary for this function you requested (Section 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG). You can delete it at any time in your browser settings.

7. Cookies, analytics and tracking

Our website does not use cookies, analytics tools or tracking services. If this changes, we will update this policy accordingly.

8. Links to external websites

Our website contains links to LinkedIn profiles. These are simple links; only when you click a link are you redirected to LinkedIn, and LinkedIn's privacy policy then applies.

9. Your rights

You have the right at any time to:

  • access (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • object (Art. 21 GDPR)
  • withdraw consent given (Art. 7(3) GDPR)

An informal message to info@heliximpulse.com is sufficient.

10. Right to lodge a complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the federal state where you live or where our company is based. The competent authority is the Hessian Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (Hessischer Beauftragter für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit).

11. Data security

The website is delivered exclusively via an encrypted connection (HTTPS). We use technical and organisational measures to protect your data against loss, misuse and unauthorised access.

12. Changes

We will update this privacy policy as soon as new functions or services are added. The version published on this page applies.
