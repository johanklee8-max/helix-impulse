About

Business strategy and marketing, brought to life

Helix Impulse is a business simulation for universities, business schools, and professional training programs. It brings business strategy and marketing to life through competitive, team-based decision-making.

Participants manage virtual companies, develop products, define their market positioning, and make strategic marketing decisions. They compete for customers and market share, experiencing how their choices influence business performance over multiple rounds.

For universities and business schools

An interactive way to teach marketing, competitive strategy, and strategic decision-making.

For companies and professional training providers

An environment where managers and teams can explore strategic choices, test their assumptions, and learn from the consequences without real-world business risk.

History

Developed from teaching experience. Designed for better learning.

Helix Impulse is developed by educators with more than 20 years of experience using business simulations in higher education. We know how valuable simulation-based learning can be, but also how challenging existing tools can be to prepare, explain, and integrate into teaching.

That's why we're taking a different approach:

Less unnecessary complexity. More focus on the decisions that matter.

Helix Impulse combines an intuitive, contemporary interface with a focused simulation model that makes business decisions and their consequences easier to understand, discuss, and evaluate.

Our goal is simple: to make business simulations easier to use, more engaging to experience, and more relevant to the people learning from them.
