About

Learning by doing – in a safe environment

In Helix Impulse, student teams run their own company and compete against each other. They make decisions, see the consequences and learn from them – without real-world risk.

01

Decide

Teams develop a marketing strategy and make the decisions of a marketing management team.

02

Compete

All teams act in the same market. Every decision affects the others.

03

Reflect

Results are discussed together – the debrief turns experience into understanding.
