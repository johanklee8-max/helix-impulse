About

Business strategy and marketing, brought to life

Helix Impulse is a business simulation for universities, business schools, and professional training programs. It brings business strategy and marketing to life through competitive, team-based decision-making.

Participants manage virtual companies, develop products, define their market positioning, and make strategic marketing decisions. They compete for customers and market share, experiencing how their choices influence business performance over multiple rounds.

History

Developed from teaching experience. Designed for better learning.

We have taught with business simulations in higher education for more than 20 years. We know what makes them valuable – and what can make them difficult to prepare, explain, and integrate into teaching.

So we built Helix Impulse around a simple idea:

Keep the strategic decisions that matter. Remove unnecessary complexity.

The result is a focused marketing and strategy simulation with an intuitive interface – designed around real teaching needs and easier to use for participants and educators alike.

Our goal is simple: to make business simulations easier to use, more engaging to experience, and more relevant to the people learning from them.
