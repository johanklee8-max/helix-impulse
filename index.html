The simulation

A market that responds to every decision

Several firms compete in the same market. Each team runs one firm and makes the decisions a marketing management team would make. At the end of every fiscal year, the simulation calculates how customers have responded – and how the competition has moved.

Analyse. Read the market: segment sizes, brand perceptions and what competitors are doing.

Decide. Plan positioning, products, price, channels, campaigns and production for the year.

Reflect. Interpret the annual results and learning feedback, then refine the strategy.

  • PositioningA positioning brief that gradually shapes how the brand is perceived.
  • ProductSound, battery life, comfort, ANC, sustainability and build quality.
  • Price & target groupWhich customers to serve – and what they are willing to pay.
  • CommunicationSocial media, influencers, search, PR, retail promotion and experiential.
  • Sales channelsSpecialty retail, mass retail, marketplace and the own online store.
  • Production & controllingCapacity, costs, product contributions and annual results.
The story

The Impulse storyline

Every round of Helix Impulse tells the same story with a different ending – written by the decisions of your students.

  1. 1

    A market in motion

    The market for in-ear headphones is crowded and growing. Your students take over a young company that wants to establish its Impulse earbuds – against established competitors fighting for the same customers.

  2. 2

    Five groups of customers

    Not everyone wants the same earbuds. Five target groups differ in their needs and their willingness to pay – and no product can be everything to everyone.

    Price-conscious buyers Commuters Audio enthusiasts Style-conscious buyers Eco-conscious buyers
  3. 3

    A brand has to be built

    Even a product that meets customer needs only sells if people know it and can buy it. Awareness, availability and a clear position take time – and development work pays off only years later.

  4. 4

    The market changes

    Over the years, trends and shocks shift what customers want. Teams have to read the signals and decide whether to adapt or stay the course.

    Sustainability wave Remote work Fitness boom Audiophile hype Downturn Tech leap
  5. 5

    The verdict of the market

    After the final fiscal year, the results speak: market share, brand strength and profit. The debrief turns them into the real lesson – why one strategy worked and another did not.

Teaching

Built to be part of your curriculum

Helix Impulse complements lectures and case studies with an experience students remember. You stay in control: you create the round, set the number of firms and fiscal years, and decide when each year is calculated.

Intensive courses

A complete simulation over one to three days. Each fiscal year is followed by a short debrief, so theory and experience alternate at a fast pace.

Seminars

One fiscal year per session over the semester. Every round can be linked to the topic of the week – from segmentation to channel strategy.

Business schools

For MBA, master's and executive education: teams of experienced participants compete on strategy, and the debrief connects decisions with management practice.

Learning outcomes

  • Segmentation & positioningAnalyse target groups, choose markets and position a brand deliberately.
  • Marketing mixCoordinate product, price, communication and distribution – and weigh the trade-offs.
  • Financial thinkingConnect marketing decisions with revenue, costs, capacity and profit over time.
  • Decisions under competitionAgree on a strategy as a team, respond to rivals and justify choices with evidence.
History

By educators, for educators

Helix Impulse was not designed in a game studio, but in the classroom. Behind it are 20 years of experience in running business games in higher education – with students, lecturers and executives.

We know what a business simulation has to deliver in teaching: a model that is easy to understand, decisions with real trade-offs, results that can be explained – and enough room for discussion in the debrief. Helix Impulse brings these two decades of practice together in a modern simulation that runs in any browser.
